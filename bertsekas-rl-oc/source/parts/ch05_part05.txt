The properties, the analysis, and the implementation of TD methods in the context of approximate PI are quite complicated. In particular, the issue of exploration is important and must be addressed. Moreover there are convergence, oscillation, and reliability issues to contend with.

LSTD(λ) relies on matrix inversion and not on iteration, so it does not have a serious convergence issue, but the system (5.49) may be singular or near singular, in which case very accurate simulation is needed to approximate C well enough for its inversion to be reliable; remedies for the case of a singular or near singular system are discussed in the papers [WaB13a], [WaB13b]
